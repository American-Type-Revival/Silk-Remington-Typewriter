Silk Remington Typewriter ATR
=============================


   *Silk Remington Typewriter ATR* is a revival of the Silk
   Remington Typewriter typeface first distributed by American
   Type Founders, expanded to include italics. **It is made for
   the exclusive use of American Type Revival and should not be
   used for other purposes.** See [license.txt][license] for more info.



   ![Silk Remington Typewriter ATR in uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and punctuation][upright]

   *The upright version of Silk Remington Typewriter ATR*


   *Silk Remington Typewriter ATR* came out of a need by
   *American Type Revival* to have a period-appropriate
   monospaced font for use on its website. It is drawn after
   Silk Remington Typewriter as sold in the 1923 American Type
   Founders specimen book, later sold under the name "Remington"
   by Linotype.


   ![Remington characters from the Linotype specimen][linotype]

   *Remington as sold by Linotype*

   Unlike other typefaces offered by the American Type Founders,
   these were only ever sold at one point size with the express
   purpose of mimicking the look of typewriters. Customers were
   encouraged to put a loosely woven cloth between the printing
   press and the paper when using this typeface to more
   accurately mimic the distressed look of authentic
   typewriters.

   Although sold to look distressed and imperfect, here this
   font is rendered cleanly to remain legible on digital
   displays at small sizes.


   As the needs of the website grew, this font was expanded to
   include italics. Because the American Type Founders never
   released an italic typewriter font, the italics are modeled
   after the much more modern [italics drawn for the IBM
   Selectric typewriter][ibm-plex].


   ![Uppercase and lowercase letters from the IBM Selectric italic specimen][selectric]

   *The IBM Selectric Typewriter Italics*


   ![The same letters in Silk Remington Typewriter ATR Italic][italic]

   *Silk Remington Typewriter ATR Italics*


   The italics are not a one-to-one copy of the IBM Selectric
   Italics. Rather, they are used as a guide for drawing italics
   for Silk Remington Typewriter ATR.


   Unlike other American Type Revival typefaces, this font is
   not designed to be used outside of internal use by ATR. It is
   made available here for download, study, and private use, but
   ATR retains the exclusive right to its use in any public
   facing capacity.
