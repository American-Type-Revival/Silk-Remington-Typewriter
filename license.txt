    PREAMBLE / SUMMARY OF THE LICENSE

    I am not proud of this font, and consequently I don't want it
    to be used in public. I designed it to be used on the
    American Type Revival website because I needed a monospaced
    typeface that felt period appropriate, but in general I
    dislike attempts to literally capture letters designed for
    typewriters (which is what this font is) because I feel that
    what looks good on a typewriter tends to translate pretty
    poorly to other mediums. I am releasing both this font and
    its source code publicly (perhaps against my better judgment)
    because I think there is a certain utility or public interest
    in being able to pick apart and understand how fonts are
    made. Therefore, you are permitted to download and tinker
    with or use this font as much as you like privately, but you
    are very much forbidden from using or distributing the font
    in any public capacity (outside of a couple of exceptions
    which I spell out below).

    When I say "the Font," I am referring to everything that I

8.  ADVICE
